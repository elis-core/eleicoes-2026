What changed in 2026

Flávio Bolsonaro won both rounds in Campos. Between rounds, Lula gained 24,822 votes and Flávio lost 1,389. In 2022, both finalists gained votes and Jair Bolsonaro widened his lead.

In 2022, a drop of 2,246 blank and invalid votes offset part of the fall in turnout of 4,582 voters, leaving 2,336 fewer valid votes. In 2026, the reduction of 7,131 blank and invalid votes exceeded the turnout drop of 3,898, and valid votes rose by 3,233. Relative to the first-round electorate, Campos recorded the largest combined drop in blank and invalid votes in Rio de Janeiro state in 2026, at 19.25 per thousand eligible voters.

Political context

In 2026, first-round candidates Cury, Caiado, Zema and Clariana backed Flávio after receiving a combined 14,695 votes in Campos. In 2022, Tebet and Ciro backed Lula after receiving 18,637 votes between them. In both elections, the other finalist gained more votes in the municipality. Endorsements help map the campaigns, and their electoral reach remains to be measured.

Lula's local campaign mobilised political figures from different parties. Its coordinators reported neighbourhood walks, student events and conversations with Flávio voters. These accounts come from the campaign. The contribution of those activities to the municipal result remains a hypothesis.

Testing the technical hypothesis

One hypothesis attributes Lula's gain to the sum of all votes for first-round candidates who were eliminated and the reduction in blank and invalid votes. Applied literally to the Campos totals, the rule would predict 27,331 additional votes (20,200 + 7,131), compared with 24,822 observed. The prediction exceeds the observed gain by 2,509. By the accounting identity, the difference equals the change in turnout minus the change in votes for Flávio.

The municipal test evaluates this literal rule. Investigating a possible software function would require a testable prediction for each polling section, reconciliation of polling station returns and records, and a controlled test of the relevant system version where access is authorised. This municipal study did not perform any of those section-level steps.
